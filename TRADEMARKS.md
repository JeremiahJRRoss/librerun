# The LibreRun name

The software is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only** ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE),
[`NOTICE`](NOTICE)), and the name is a separate matter. The licence
governs what you may do with the code: use it, study it, change it, fork
it and share it, on its terms. It gives no permission to present a fork,
a service or a modified version as the official LibreRun project, and
nothing on this page adds a term to the licence or takes one away.

"LibreRun" names the official project, which Jeremiah Ross maintains.
This page says how to use the name so that nobody is misled about whose
software they are running. It relies on no trademark registration and
claims none.

## You do not need to ask to

- **Fork, modify and share the software**, on the AGPL's terms. That is
  what the licence is for.
- **Say truthfully what your software is or does.** "A fork of
  LibreRun", "based on LibreRun", "works with LibreRun", "compatible with
  LibreRun", "a LibreRun agent", "built for LibreRun". Accurate
  statements of fact, compatibility statements and other nominative uses
  are not restricted by this page, and neither is anything else the law
  allows.
- **Redistribute unmodified LibreRun under its own name**, commercially
  or not, on the AGPL's terms.
- **Name an integration after what it does**, in a way that shows it is
  yours: `acme-librerun-adapter`, "Acme Agents for LibreRun".
- **Teach, review, compare and criticise.** Write the book, give the
  talk, publish the benchmark.

## Please don't

- **Imply endorsement or official status.** Don't present a fork, a
  hosted service, a modified version or a product as the official
  LibreRun, or as published, sponsored, certified or endorsed by the
  project, unless it is.
- **Keep the name where it would confuse.** If you distribute a modified
  LibreRun, or run one as a service, and people could mistake it for the
  official project, give it a name that is clearly your own: "Acme
  Runner, derived from LibreRun", not "LibreRun by Acme". Someone who
  reports a bug should know which project to report it to.
- **Build your own brand on the name** — a product, company, service or
  domain called "LibreRun", or something close enough to be taken for
  it ("LibreRun Cloud", "LibreRunHQ", "Libre Run") — where that would
  cause the confusion above.

## Package and image names

The project publishes source only: no package or image goes out under
the LibreRun name. `librerun`, `librerun-agent`, `librerun-langgraph`
and the image names in `compose.yaml` are the names of the project's
own components in this repository. If you publish a package or image
built from LibreRun, name it so that nobody believes it came from the
project.

## How to ask

If you are not sure whether a use is fine, write to
**dev@librerun.dev**, or open an issue titled "Name question". Say who
you are, what you want to call it, and what it is.

## If this page is wrong

This policy exists to stop confusion, not to stop use. If it reads as
though it forbids something the community should plainly be allowed to
do — a conference, a user group, a t-shirt, a blog series — that is a
defect in the policy. Open an issue and say so.
